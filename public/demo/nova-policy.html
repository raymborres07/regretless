Nova Electronics demo price-adjustment policy

For this fictional demo, Nova Electronics permits one post-purchase price adjustment within 14 calendar days of purchase when the identical model and variant is listed at a lower public price by Nova Electronics.

The potential adjustment is the difference between the original unit price and the current unit price, multiplied by the purchased quantity. Taxes and shipping are excluded.

New Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones purchased directly from Nova Electronics are included. No membership is required. Marketplace purchases, used products, coupon-only discounts and clearance offers are excluded.

Required proof: order number, purchase date, original price and link to the current product listing. Requests require merchant approval. These test terms do not establish any actual entitlement.
